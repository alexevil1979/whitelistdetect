Name: Whitelist Pulse

Short description (EN, 80 chars):
See if your connection looks like allowlist, regular internet, or offline

Full description (EN):
Whitelist Pulse diagnoses your current network: regular internet, allowlist-like restrictions, no connectivity, VPN-skewed results, likely abroad/bypass, or mixed/unstable answers.

This is a diagnostic tool. It does not bypass restrictions, is not a VPN, and is not affiliated with ministries, carriers, or regulators.

Features:
• On-device HTTPS checks of control site groups
• Plain-language verdict
• Network type, VPN hints, GeoIP, DNS
• Custom sites, home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile
• Optional status-bar monitor (compact check every minute)
• Russian and English, no telemetry, history stays on device

Site lists are an unofficial public-data snapshot and may go stale.
